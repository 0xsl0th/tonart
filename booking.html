03 / BEFORE YOU BOOK

A few useful
details.

When does the turnaround start?

After payment, your brief, and usable final files are received and we confirm the scope. Business days exclude weekends and public holidays. Times shown are for a first preview, not the entire revision process.

What if my project is outside the package scope?

Please message us before paying if the track is longer than 10 minutes, has more tracks or stems than the offer allows, or needs editing, tuning, production or alternate versions.

Is the booking instant?

Payment is immediate. We confirm the project after checking the payment, files and availability. If the package does not fit, we will offer a revised quote or a full refund before work begins.

How do you match my payment to my music?

After paying, use the project handover page. Enter the email used at checkout or shown on your receipt, your buyer transaction ID if available, and a private downloadable link to your files. We check these details against the payment before confirming your project.

These are introductory launch rates. Included revisions cover feedback on the supplied material; new recordings or major arrangement changes are a new scope. Your statutory rights are unaffected.

04 / ORDER POLICIES

Clear expectations
for both sides.

Cancellations & refunds

If you cancel before work begins, or if we cannot accept your files within the package scope, we will refund the payment in full. Once work has started, contact us to resolve the issue; any refund is assessed under applicable law and the work already delivered. Your statutory rights remain intact.

Privacy & your music

This website stores your language choice in your browser and loads fonts from Google. PayPal processes payments; your email provider carries project details; your chosen file-sharing service hosts the audio. We use your contact and payment details, project brief and audio to work on your project. We do not publish your music without permission. You can request deletion of project files after delivery by email.
