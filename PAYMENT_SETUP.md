## Mercado Pago Argentina and direct transfers

Mercado Pago Argentina's hosted links collect **ARS**, whereas the public offer list and PayPal links are in **USD**. The operator chose [DolarHoy dólar blue venta](https://dolarhoy.com/cotizaciondolarblue) for conversion. The booking page calculates the Mercado Pago amount as `round(USD offer price × ARS/USD selling rate × 1.10)` and a direct transfer amount as `round(USD offer price × ARS/USD selling rate)`. The 10% adjustment applies only to the payment link. The source rate and update time appear beside the ARS amounts. At setup, DolarHoy showed **ARS 1,565/USD**; the current source timestamp is in `booking.js` and on the live page. The site hides the amounts and direct Mercado Pago button 24 hours after that source update, leaving quote requests available.

An amount-free Tonart Mercado Pago link is connected to the booking page. Its buyer page asks the customer to enter the amount, so the customer must enter the **exact displayed ARS amount** while the rate is current; check the amount received before starting. A quote is still available if the source rate is stale or the customer wants confirmation. Mercado Pago [documents amount-free and fixed-amount links](https://www.mercadopago.com.ar/herramientas-para-vender/link-de-pago). Its buyer preview displays **Enrique Herranz**; the operator chose to keep that business display name, and the booking page tells customers to expect it.

For a direct transfer, the operator authorized publishing the receiving Mercado Pago alias on the booking page. The customer transfers the **exact displayed base ARS amount** while the rate is current, or requests a fresh quote if it is stale. A transfer to the alias/CVU is separate from hosted checkout; Mercado Pago's [Argentina payment-method table](https://www.mercadopago.com.ar/developers/es/docs/sales-processing/payment-methods) does not list bank transfer as a checkout method. Do not publish the full CVU without a separate decision. The buyer should send the credited amount, sender name and operation reference in the handover. Verify the incoming credit and exact ARS amount in Mercado Pago Activity; an email or transfer receipt alone does not prove funds arrived.

The [refresh workflow](.github/workflows/refresh-ars-rate.yml) checks DolarHoy at **11:00 and 22:00 Buenos Aires time**. Its [parser](scripts/refresh-ars-rate.mjs) reads the blue selling quote and source timestamp from the public page, verifies a plausible rate, a source timestamp no older than 48 hours and a change no larger than 10% without manual review, then updates only the rate and timestamp in `booking.js`. A changed file is committed to `main` and an explicit GitHub Pages build is requested. A failed check leaves the prior site in place; once its displayed rate reaches 24 hours old, the direct ARS amounts disappear and customers can request a quote. Run `node --test scripts/refresh-ars-rate.test.mjs` before changing the parser. To update manually, download the current DolarHoy blue page and run `node scripts/refresh-ars-rate.mjs --html-file PATH`; review the resulting diff before pushing. A rate jump over 10% requires checking the source and manually dispatching the workflow with `allow_large_change` enabled.

## Project handover and payment matching

The static site does not receive payment webhooks or verify payments. After paying, the buyer uses `handover.html` to prepare an email with the chosen service and payment method, contact and receipt details, artist and track title, and a private downloadable file link. For Mercado Pago and transfers, it asks for the ARS amount; for transfers, it also asks for the sender account name. The buyer must press **Send** in their mail app; opening the handover page or generating an email draft does not submit an order. The site does not store project details or host audio. A PayPal guest-card buyer may not have an Activity page from which to copy an ID, so their payer email remains the main PayPal matching field.

For each handover email, search PayPal Activity by buyer transaction ID when supplied, then by payer email and payment date/title if necessary. PayPal gives buyer and seller [different transaction IDs](https://www.paypal.com/de/cshelp/article/warum-gibt-es-f%C3%BCr-dieselbe-transaktion-unterschiedliche-transaktionscodes-von-k%C3%A4ufern-und-verk%C3%A4ufern-ts2265); do not expect the buyer code to equal the seller-side receipt ID. Check that the transaction is **Completed**, its USD total and item match the selected offer, and that the link contains the correct number of usable files. Record the seller transaction ID, client email, service, file link, and agreed first-preview date in an internal project log. Reply to the customer to confirm receipt and the date. If matching fails, ask for the PayPal receipt email or buyer transaction ID before starting work. The first-preview clock starts after payment, project scope, and usable files are confirmed. A return to this site from PayPal is not proof of payment. PayPal documents the [Activity report fields](https://developer.paypal.com/reports/activity-download/) and [payment statuses](https://securepayments.paypal.com/de/cshelp/article/welche-bedeutung-hat-der-status-meiner-zahlung-oder-geldanforderung-in-meinem-paypal-konto-help668).

## Checkout language

Tonart remembers the visitor's English, Spanish or German selection, but PayPal's hosted Payment Links do not inherit that setting. PayPal says a prior `LANG` cookie can override the browser's language. It documents a checkout-language override only with **both** `locale.x` and `country.x` in the link; that country override also makes PayPal treat every visitor as a buyer from the specified country. Because Tonart serves international buyers, the links keep PayPal's automatic buyer-region handling rather than deriving a buyer country from the site's language selector. Merchant-entered product names and descriptions remain in their original language. See [PayPal's Payment Links troubleshooting guide](https://developer.paypal.com/payment-links-buttons/troubleshooting/) and [PayPal Help](https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/paypal-pay-links-and-button-troubleshooting-tips-ts2341).
